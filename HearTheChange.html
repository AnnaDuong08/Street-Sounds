Hear the change

More and more vendors play a recording from a loudspeaker instead of calling themselves. Compare the two: the live voice bends and drifts with the tones of the words, while the recording repeats exactly and the cheap speaker cuts off the warmth.

How to compare

Choose a call, then press Live voice and Loudspeaker loop in turn. Watch the waveform: the live voice moves and changes, the loudspeaker version is harsher and identical every time.

Vietnamese is tonal, so the melody of a call comes from its words. Each melody here follows the tone marks in the call's text.

Uses your device's Vietnamese voice, if it has one.
