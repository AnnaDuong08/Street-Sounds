Tiếng Rao

The sung calls of Vietnam's street vendors, before loudspeakers and delivery apps replace them.

Meet the vendors

Six sellers, six calls. Each one is a small piece of music: the melody comes from the tones of the Vietnamese words.

Auntie Lan

Bánh mì nóng giòn đây!

Hot, crispy bread rolls here!

Rides from alley to alley on an old green bicycle every evening.

Grandma Sáu

Hột vịt lộn đây!

Balut eggs here!

Has carried the same shoulder pole for forty years and sells late into the night.

Hoa

Ai ăn chè không?

Who wants sweet soup?

Pushes a glass-sided cart so you can choose your chè by colour.

Tâm

Lốc cốc… lốc cốc…

The knock of the noodle runners

Calls with two bamboo sticks instead of his voice, then runs the bowls up the stairs.

Auntie Thu

Ai bánh giò nóng đây!

Who wants hot rice dumplings?

Walks Hà Nội's Old Quarter on winter nights with a steaming basket.

Uncle Hùng

Bánh bao nóng hổi đây!

Piping-hot steamed buns here!

Plays his call from a loudspeaker now, and secretly misses calling it himself.

Five ways to listen

Pick where to start. Every page works on its own, and the top menu takes you between them.

Night walk Scroll along a row of stalls. The vendor in the middle is loudest; the rest fade off to the left and right, like a real alley. Open night walk →
Hear the change Compare a vendor singing their call with the same call played from a cheap loudspeaker, and watch the waveform change. Open hear the change →
Mix your street Drag vendors and street sounds onto a board and hear them from where you stand. Save it or share it as a code. Open mix your street →
Your street Walk your street in a small game: talk to vendors, buy food, ride a motorbike and run deliveries. Open your street →
Add a call Know a call from your own city? Send in the words, what it means and, if you have one, a recording. Open add a call →

Tiếng Rao is a CSCU9X5 web design prototype built with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript. The six vendor calls are real street recordings, played and mixed with the Web Audio API. The street sounds (motorbikes, rain, temple bell and crickets) are still generated live in the browser.

Saved streets, purchases and submitted calls are stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
