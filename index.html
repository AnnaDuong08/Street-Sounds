01 · Walk

Night walk

Turn the sound on and scroll along the street. Vendors close to the middle of the screen are loud; the ones further away fade into the background, left and right, like they would in a real alley.

Sound is off. Press "start walking" or tap any stall to hear its call.

02 · Listen

Hear the change

More and more vendors play a recording from a loudspeaker instead of calling themselves. Compare the two. The live voice bends and drifts with the tones of the words. The recording repeats exactly, every time, and the cheap speaker cuts off the warmth.

Vietnamese is tonal, so the melody of a call comes from the words. This prototype builds each call's melody from the tone marks in its text.

Uses your device's Vietnamese voice, if it has one.

03 · Play

Mix your street

Build your own soundscape. Add vendors and street sounds, then drag them around. The closer a sound is to you, the louder and clearer it gets. There's no score and no timer.

    Tiếng Rao is a CSCU9X5 web design prototype built with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript. All sound is generated live with the Web Audio API. Each call's melody follows the six Vietnamese tones of its words (level, rising, falling, dipping, broken and heavy). The final site will replace these placeholders with field recordings, or Creative Commons recordings with credits.

    Saved mixes and submitted calls are stored only in this browser.
